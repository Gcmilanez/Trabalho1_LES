No terminal foram executados os seguintes comandos:

Foram compilados todos os programas utilizando um arquivo MakeFile e o comando "make"

1 - g++ -std=c++17 -O3 generate_data.cpp -o generate_data

2 - ./generate_data

3 - sudo sysctl -w kernel.perf_event_paranoid=0

4 - E="cache-references,cache-misses,L1-dcache-loads,L1-dcache-load-misses,l2_cache_accesses_from_dc_misses,l2_cache_misses_from_dc_misses,cycles,instructions,branches,branch-misses"





Foram utilizados esses datasets para os testes em árvores:
http://snap.stanford.edu/data/soc-Slashdot0811.txt.gz 77,360 nodes | 905,468 edges
https://snap.stanford.edu/data/email-EuAll.txt.gz 1,005 nodes | 25,571 edgee
https://snap.stanford.edu/data/as-skitter.txt.gz  1,696,415 nodes | 11,095,298 edges
https://snap.stanford.edu/data/gplus_combined.txt.gz  107,614 nodes | 13,673,453 edges
https://snap.stanford.edu/data/bigdata/communities/com-orkut.all.cmty.txt.gz  3,072,441 nodes | 117,185,083 edges
